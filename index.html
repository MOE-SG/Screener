A heuristic score, not a prediction or advice. It favours fast growth, strong momentum, smaller size and a wide trading range. Leveraged ETFs are flagged and left out of the ranking, since they can double from leverage alone.

Setup x/4 counts four entry checks: (1) price above a rising 50-day and 200-day average, (2) breaking out of a sideways base (range ≤ 20% over 25 days) on ≥ 1.5× average volume, or sitting within 5% of the breakout level, (3) no more than 10% above the 50-day average, (4) no earnings in the next 7 days. During US trading hours today's bar is still forming, so its volume reads low.
